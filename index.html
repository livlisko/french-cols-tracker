2.
Or download the file Right-click the page and choose "Save As" to keep a copy on your computer.

Challenge de la Ligne Bleue

41 cols along the historic 1871-1918 Franco-German border

Historical context: This challenge traces la ligne bleue des Vosges, the symbolic frontier that emerged after the Treaty of Frankfurt (1871), when most of Alsace and part of Lorraine were annexed by the newly created German Empire. For the next 47 years, many of these cols lay on or near the border between France and the Reichsland Alsace-Lorraine, with the Vosges ridge widely perceived as the dividing line between the "lost provinces" and the French Republic. The territory was recovered following the Allied victory in the First World War, and its return to French sovereignty was formalized by the Treaty of Versailles in 1919.
